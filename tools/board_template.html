How to interpret this view

Change is descriptive. Each cell subtracts the previous reading of the same model and battery. Pass-rate differences are percentage points. Teal means an increase; rust means a decrease. Neither colour is a quality judgment.

Published findings are separate. This view does not assign Movement, Watch, or Divergence status. Those labels come from the instrument’s published findings and statistical procedure (the advisories and divergence feeds). A visible jump is not sufficient evidence on its own.

Different batteries stay separate. Daily and weekly results use different probe batteries. Weekly observations appear at their actual dates. The top-level models field in the served series JSON repeats the daily series and is not counted again.

Gaps remain gaps. A hatched cell means the requested value is null. Empty dates have no reading for that model. A white cell in Change mode means there is no usable previous comparison, including a roster revision. Errors can coexist with a 100% pass rate among the graded responses.

History needs provenance. The board includes roster versions and the latest battery hash, but not the full history of settings, probe composition, denominators, or grading changes. Roster revisions are marked; unrecorded method changes cannot be resolved here.

Readings, not a composite rating. Dimension traces retain the individual signals, never a single score. Cost is an estimate for the run, and zero cost during missing behavioural readings is not evidence of efficiency.
